Wide camera

Off

A fixed USB camera beside the PTZ camera. Its view never moves, so it keeps counting while the PTZ follows someone, and it can show the PTZ where to look.

Part of the camera's name as the Pi lists it (e.g. Avaya HC020), or a /dev/v4l/by-id/ path. Changing it restarts detection.

Every event keeps the PTZ camera's clip. "Only when they add something" keeps the wide clip when, during the event, the PTZ moved, followed someone or was offline, the wide camera was main, or the PTZ's own clip failed. Otherwise it shows the same scene and only costs storage.

If lamps flicker or roll through the picture, match your mains: 50 Hz in most of the world, 60 Hz in the Americas. Applied at once, and put back whenever the camera resets it.

Camera sync–

The sync tells the PTZ where to point for any spot in the wide picture. Measure it once in daylight with the app stopped (about 3 minutes; the PTZ sweeps its whole view), and again whenever either camera is moved. In the app's folder on the Pi, run:

sudo systemctl stop ptzcam .venv/bin/python tools/calibrate_sync.py sudo systemctl start ptzcam

Or let the Pi do it by itself at the next clear daytime moment (it waits until both pictures have been clear for 10 minutes, then stops the app for those 3 minutes; Settings shows its progress here):

sudo systemd-run --unit=ptzcam-sync-once /home/pi/ptz_cam/.venv/bin/python /home/pi/ptz_cam/tools/sync_when_clear.py
